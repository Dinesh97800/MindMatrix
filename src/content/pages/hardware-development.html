What PCB design tools do you use? expand_more

Do you handle high-volume manufacturing? expand_more

While we are a design firm, we provide full support for hand-off to Tier 1 and Tier 2 contract manufacturers, including supply chain management and manufacturing engineering support.

Can you support EMI/EMC pre-compliance? expand_more

Yes. Our labs are equipped for initial pre-compliance scanning to identify potential issues before you commit to expensive third-party testing labs.
