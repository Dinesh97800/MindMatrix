Technical FAQs

How do you handle model drift in edge environments? expand_more
We implement federated learning and automated re-training triggers. When a distribution shift is detected locally, anonymized edge-cases are securely uploaded for model refinement and redeployed via OTA updates.
What is the typical latency for your computer vision pipelines? expand_more
Our standard object detection pipelines on NVIDIA Jetson Orin modules achieve sub-5ms end-to-end latency, making them suitable for high-speed industrial safety and sorting applications.
Do you support legacy industrial hardware? expand_more
Yes. We use ruggedized IoT gateways that bridge legacy PLC systems (RS232/RS485) with modern AI compute modules, providing a "digital brain" to existing industrial assets.
