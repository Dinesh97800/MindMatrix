The Hardware Ceiling

Standard DAQ systems were fundamentally too slow to capture high-frequency transients occurring during the transonic phase of the Hyperloop pod's journey. At 1000km/h, even millisecond-level jitter obscures critical structural integrity data, making flight safety verification impossible.

Extreme Vibration

Maintaining mechanical connection and signal integrity at peak acoustic resonance.

Distributed Sync

The Innovation

Distributed Sensor Mesh with FPGA Core Validation

We developed a custom distributed wireless sensor network utilizing Xilinx Artix-7 FPGA cores. By shifting signal processing from software to hardware logic, we achieved deterministic time-stamping at the point of ingestion.
